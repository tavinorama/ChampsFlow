## 1. How to run your Free AI Visibility Test

1. Go to **[ozvor.com/test](https://ozvor.com/test)**.
2. Enter your **website**, **brand**, **category**, and one **competitor**.
3. Add your **email** — your results are saved to it (and, once you create an
   account with that email, your test comes with you).
4. Click **Run**. Ozvor queries ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI
   Overview *live* — about 60 seconds.
5. You'll see your **Ozvor AI Visibility Score**, the exact buyer prompt asked,
   and which engines cited you vs your competitor.

*If a run times out, just run it again — we show an honest gap, never a made-up
number.*

## 2. How to buy (and open) the Get-Cited Kit

1. From your free-test results or **[ozvor.com/kit](https://ozvor.com/kit)**, click
   **Get the Kit — $29**.
2. Complete secure checkout (one-time payment). You can sign in with Google /
   GitHub / LinkedIn so the Kit is saved to your account.
3. You'll get a **delivery email** from `hello@ozvor.com` with your private Kit
   link, and land on your **Kit page**.
4. On the Kit page you can **download** the Kit PDF and the companion guide, and
   read your audit, top-3 fixes, and 3 drafts.
5. Lost the link? Email **support@ozvor.com** from your purchase email and we'll
   re-send it — no second charge.

## 3. How to publish your drafts

1. Open your 3 drafts (blog post, LinkedIn post, FAQ/schema block) in the Kit.
2. Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]` with your **real facts** — we never invent
   numbers, so those blanks are intentional.
3. Publish each where the **"where to publish" checklist** says: the FAQ/schema on
   your site, the proof post on LinkedIn, the article on your blog.
4. Give AI engines time to re-crawl, then **re-test in ~30 days** (article 4).

## 4. How to re-test in 30 days

1. AI answers move week to week, so a single audit is a snapshot.
2. Use your **30-day re-test voucher** (in the Kit) or just run the free test
   again with the same brand.
3. Compare your new score to the first one to see whether your published fixes
   moved the needle.
4. Want this automatic? **Growth** re-audits weekly and alerts you when your score
   or a competitor's position changes.

  the next renewal; you keep access to the end of the current period.
- **Kit refund:** if your 3 drafts aren't ready to publish in ~10 minutes, we
  refund the $29 — we guarantee the deliverable, never AI behavior.
- **Plan refund:** Growth and Agency are covered by a **30-day money-back
  guarantee**.
- **AI Audit Stack ($49) refund:** 30-day money-back — if the result tells you
  nothing new, we refund the $49 (article 6).
- **Ozvor Pages ($99) refund:** if your 5-page site is not live and ready to
  publish, we refund the $99 (article 7).
- To request any of these, email **support@ozvor.com** (the one address for all
  refunds) from your purchase email with "refund" or "cancel" and we'll take
  care of it. Full policy: [ozvor.com/refund](https://ozvor.com/refund).

## 7. Ozvor Pages ($99) — what you get and how it is delivered

1. Ozvor Pages is a one-time $99 purchase at
   **[ozvor.com/local-pages](https://ozvor.com/local-pages)** (Growth includes
   the Pages builder at no extra cost).
2. We build a **5-page, AI-citable site** (home, services, about, FAQ, contact)

   filler.

   You can regenerate a site a limited number of times (2 per purchased site;
   Growth/Agency: 5 per site per month).

5. What we do NOT promise: same-day social publishing, page-speed records, or
   any citation/traffic result.

## 8. Credits — how the monthly reset and packs work

1. Every paid plan gets a monthly allowance of audit credits (an audit spends
   credits by depth: 50 credits per prompt).
2. **Plan credits reset on the 1st of every month (UTC).** What is left of last
   month's plan allowance **expires** — it does not roll over.
3. **Purchased credit packs never expire.** A pack (1,000 credits, price shown
   at checkout — about $13) stays in your balance until you use it, across
   months.
4. When you spend credits, plan credits are consumed first and pack credits
   last, so a pack is never burned while plan credits are still available.
5. See your balance and buy a pack at **Account → Billing** (the credits pill
   on the dashboard shows the same number).

## 9. The chat widget — what it can and cannot do

1. The chat bubble on ozvor.com is an **AI assistant** (it says so in the
   window). It answers questions about products, pricing, plans and how the
   audit works, in your language, and points you to the right page.
2. It **cannot** see your account, run an audit, change your plan, or issue a
   refund. For anything account-specific, it will send you to
   **support@ozvor.com** or to **[/book](https://ozvor.com/book)** (a call with
   the founder).
3. Do not paste passwords, card numbers or other people's personal data into
   the chat. We do not need them and the assistant will not ask for them.
4. If the assistant says something that contradicts a page on the site, the
   page (and the Terms of Service) wins — tell us at support@ozvor.com so we can
   fix it.
